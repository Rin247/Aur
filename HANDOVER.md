## Editor defect sweep (plan: `Bible/notes/editsweep.md`)

D1 (13 audited bugs), D2.S1 (tile palette), D3.S1 (entity selection), D4.S1b
(the QUndoStack rewrite) and D4.S2 (the owner's two visual reports) all
landed. D4.S1b1 corrected three regions of the shared base that resolved
shapes the project does not have; D4.S1b2 moved the map editor onto per-cell
diffs; D4.S1e rewrote the command-core tests against the real shapes;
D4.S2 fixed the map editor's first-boot zoom, the world's black bars and the
tool combo, and added the render harness.

**D5.S1, the sprite studio, landed** (four commits): onion skinning (the
neighbouring frames ghosted behind the current one), a live preview of the
sprite's own sequence at the anim's fps, the mirror (the right half onto its
left), and the frame tools (add, duplicate, delete, reorder). The sprite's
tools live in their own row under the toolbar; the graphic toolbar was
clipping their labels. The structural edits are pure functions over the
graphic JSON in `studio/PixelFrameOps.{hpp,cpp}`, so they are tested without
a widget.

**D6.S1, tile pixel editing, landed** (three commits): `studio/PixelSurface`
is the one pixel-editing surface — it edits "a rows array with a palette",
which is a graphic's frame and a tile — and `studio/PixelCanvas` is the
binding that points it at a frame. The tileset editor has the canvas, the
tile's own palette as the colour picker, and Export PNG / Import PNG (every
imported pixel snapped to the nearest palette colour, a pure function).

**D7.S1 to D7.S3, the chrome, landed**: the file menu is a hamburger in the
title bar, the project's buttons are icon buttons beside it, and the tab bar
sits in the same row (the pages are a stack below it), so the two rows the
editors were losing are gone. `WM_NCCALCSIZE` gives the client area the
whole window, so the chrome reaches the screen edge and there is no OS border
to drag (the edge resize was already there in `WM_NCHITTEST`). Every canvas
fits the space it is given: the map, the world and the pixel surface scale to
the pane with a small margin.

The world's second tab is "Settings" (it was "Time & World Settings").

Next open phase: none in the editsweep — D5, D6 and D7 are closed. The
owner's queue is in `WANTED.md`.

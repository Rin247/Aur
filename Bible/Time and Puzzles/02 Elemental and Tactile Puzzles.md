## 🕯️ THE ALUNDRA LAYER

**1. Tactile Rooms.** Ice-slide floors with pillar-stops, current-conveyors, timed switch chains, block-push plates (your music puzzles already live here). One non-negotiable QoL rule in your philosophy: **instant one-button room reset.** Alundra punished retries; we keep the challenge, not the friction.

**2. Umbral Regrets (the dream-dive analogue).** At scar-sites, shift into the Umbral layer and meet a spirit echo reliving a frozen moment. The puzzle: change **one variable** in the echo — move an object, stop a person, ring a bell on the right beat — to resolve the regret. Resolution rewrites a small piece of the Aetheric world (a door unbars, a bridge remembers itself). This is Alundra's psychological layer fused with your ALttP-style shift, and it's *lore delivery disguised as mechanics*.

**3. The Dare Tier.** Post-game "Alundra Rooms": no hints, precision timing, multi-step chains. Rewards: bragging achievements, leaderboard clear-times, and the rarest cosmetics. Failure costs nothing but pride and seconds.

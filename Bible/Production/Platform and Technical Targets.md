## 13. PLATFORM & TECHNICAL TARGETS

- **Engine:** Godot 4.x (GDScript primary)
- **Platforms:** Steam (Windows + Linux). No macOS.
- **API:** GodotSteam (Achievements, Cloud Saves, Leaderboards, Workshop)
- **Dialogue:** Dialogic 2
- **Camera:** Phantom Camera plugin
- **AI:** LimboAI (Behavior Trees)
- **Terrain:** Terrain3D + GridMap + CSG
- **Map:** Wonderdraft (external, 8K export max)
- **DAW:** Bitwig Studio
- **Sprites:** Aseprite / Pixelorama

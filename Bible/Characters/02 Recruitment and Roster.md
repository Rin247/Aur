## 🎭 RECRUITMENT DOCTRINE (The "Meet & Greet" Rules)

1. **Everyone exists first as a hub NPC.** Full 48-minute-clock schedule, banter lines, visible instrument, visible job tells. You can play the whole game and never invite a single one of them.
2. **Invitation is player-initiated, acceptance is conditional.** Creed tier, corruption level, completed PA chains, gifts, duels, translations — all gates live on *their* side of the conversation.
3. **No story gate requires any recruit.** Bosses and era arcs are designed around "MC + any three." Dialogic swaps companion barks per present roster; absent characters get referenced, not required.
4. **Reserves keep living.** Unrecruited and reserved characters continue their schedules, age through eras, and appear in the **credits vignette matrix** — a slide per character showing their fate *whether or not you ever took them with you*. That's your replay bait.
5. **Roster cap: MC + 7 aboard, 4 active in combat.** Ten recruitable candidates exist per playthrough. The math guarantees misses. That's the point.

## 🗝️ RECRUITMENT DOSSIERS (Conditions, Refusals, Missables)

**Kaelen** — Found anchoring a fractured leyline node in the Sylvani highlands. Joins if you solve the node's chord puzzle *without* forcing it. **Permanent refusal if:** the node is corrupted by your hand, or your `paradox_level > 0` — he reads causality scars the way others read scent, and he will quietly close his eyes and say *"The bough does not bend for the unwalked day."* Chills, every time.

**Thalassa** — Brine-Master heir running a ledger of everyone's secrets. Three-PA chain; she joins if you trade her something genuinely irreplaceable, or if you expose her ledger and she decides you're more useful owned than owed. **Mid-game departure risk:** take a Tier 3 Harmony choice that collapses her biggest deal and she leaves the roster with a polite note and your best weapon. Star Ocean-level drama, earned.

**Dasha** — Forge-Tide duel window in Era 2/3/4. Win clean or lose gracefully: both recruit her (she respects synergy, not victory). **Refusal if:** you win while corruption > 50, or a paradox event fires mid-duel — she smells the cheat even if the rules don't.

**Sorel** — The missable-with-teeth one. A storm-downed fledgling whose clutch-egg is spoiling: **two clock phases** to return it intact. Miss it and he does *not* lock out — he joins later through a mourning PA chain, but his Dandere burst becomes **Grief Surge** (same role, self-damage risk, different dialogue tree). Consequence without punishment, exactly your QoL philosophy.

**Kessa** — Whelping event, **Night phase, Era 3 only**. Help deliver the litter and she joins; walk past and the den closes. **Refusal if:** taint > 60 — she noses your wrist once and says *"You smell like deep water. Come back when you smell like rain."*

**Dragomir** — Always available, never automatic. Cook him a dish he rates four stars (likes-matching minigame) and he closes the tavern mid-shift to follow you. The only recruit whose gate is purely *kindness and competence*.

**Meilin** — Gate: `translation_level ≥ 2`. She's decoding the same precursor tomes you are, and she will not travel with someone who can't read the footnotes. Forbidden-meridian techniques; masks a purged-clan trauma behind flirtation and terrible puns.

**Thalassa ↔ Nerin convergence** — Nerin owes Thalassa a debt. You can recruit **both** only on the hard social route: Harmony Tier 2 + a Pure Aether-Salt gift to settle the debt publicly. Otherwise it's one or the other — his chain or her ledger. One exclusivity pair per playthrough, resolved by play, not by coin.

**Sevi** — Era 5 only. Requires the Era 4 extraction-camp liberation flag *and* corruption < 20 when you meet her. She flinches at taint auras; a blighted protagonist terrifies her into silence. Her PA chain ends with her choosing a real elven name — **Seviran** — and asking you to stop calling her by the serial.

**Korrath-Vael** — Post-True Ending, Chorus puzzle, NG+ carryover via GlobalProfile. The secret ending's secret character, exactly as architected.

## 👥 TEMPORARY & NON-RECRUITABLE

- **Rik Valdris** joins for two story chapters (Era 3 capital arc, Era 5 reunion). Never permanent — council duty. His Terra/Zephyr gravity-weave covers tank gaps during those arcs, and his PAs run regardless.
- **Aunt Sigrune** never joins. The "Invite" button in her dialogue is **permanently greyed out** with the tooltip: *"She pretends not to see the button."* Running gag across all three games.

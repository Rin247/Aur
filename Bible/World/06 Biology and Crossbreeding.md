## 11. CROSSBREEDING RULES

| Pairing | Offspring | Notes |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| Sylvani × Solani | Hybrid elf. Mixed traits. | Historically rare (~1–2%). Post-timeskip: 8–12%. Called `Vaelen-Kai` or `Dune-Sylvani`. |
| Elf × Human | Half-elf. Inherits progenitor phenotype. | Longevity retained. Mana affinity diluted. |
| Human × Beastkin | Hybrid. Mammalian traits. | Rare but possible. |
| Human × Featherfolk | Hybrid. Wing development varies. | Often vestigial wings. |
| Orc × Elf/Human | Halfling. Stuck with progenitor phenotype. | Usually unwilling. Cultural stigma. |
| Dragon × Any race | 50/50 full-blood or half-breed. | The Ordeal applies. Massive mana reserves in hybrids. |
| Dragon × Dragon | Full-blooded. | Only dragons can breed with dragons and produce stable lineage. |
| Were/Vampire × Any | Strain inheritance. | Recessive. Rare. Can spread via bite/fluid. |

**Halfling Rule:** Halflings are stuck with their progenitor race's phenotype. They cannot crossbreed further *except* dragons and dragon-halflings.

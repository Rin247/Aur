# 📘 BIBLE I: GAME DESIGN DOCUMENT
### *"How It Plays, Feels, and Breaks"*

---

## 1. DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

**Core Pillars (in priority order):**

| # | Pillar | Meaning |
|---|--------|---------|
| 1 | **Respect the Player's Time** | Pause anywhere. Save anywhere. No punishment for stepping away. No forced grinds. Class swaps are free and instant. |
| 2 | **Physicality Over Abstraction** | Battles happen where you stand. Enemies are visible. Dodging is a real action. The camera *swoops*, the world *dissolves*, the music *shifts*. Nothing is a menu screen. |
| 3 | **Systemic Interconnection** | The 48-minute clock, the hydration meter, the leyline tides, the affinity bonds—every system touches every other system. No isolated mechanics. |
| 4 | **Earned Discovery** | Secrets are *hidden*, not gated. The 4th ending requires genuine exploration, not a checklist. The Al Bhed language decodes through curiosity, not fetch quests. |
| 5 | **Subverted Expectations** | Gender tropes flipped. Class stereotypes inverted. The "bad" ending is a pun. The "real" ending plays *after* the credits. The protagonist's flaw is her versatility. |

**Inspirational Anchors:**
- *Chrono Trigger* → Time travel structure, dual-techs, multiple endings, the feeling of a small world with enormous depth
- *Star Ocean* series → Scale of exploration, sci-fi/fantasy blend, real-time overworld encounters, party banter
- *Octopath Traveler* → 2.5D visual language, job swapping, individual character arcs converging
- *Triangle Strategy* → Tactical weight, consequence-driven choices, terrain as strategy
- *Trials of Mana* → Real-time fluidity, class evolution, elemental pairing
- *Clair Obscur: Expedition 33* → Shared-grid combat, seamless transitions
- *Golden Sun* → Dynamic camera, overworld interaction, puzzle integration
- *Nier: Automata* → Ending structure as narrative device, the "joke" ending as design statement

**Scale Reference:** Star Ocean 2/3 hub density. Not open-world sprawl. Dense, handcrafted hubs connected by a traversable world map. Each hub should feel like a *place*, not a waypoint.

## 2. THE THREE GAMES — STRUCTURAL OVERVIEW

| | **Game 1: Turn-Based** | **Game 2: Tactical** | **Game 3: Real-Time** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Combat Model** | Chrono Trigger spatial slots | Triangle Strategy / Fire Emblem grid | Trials of Mana / Tales of fluid action |
| **Time Mechanic** | Timegates (Stargate-style portals) | Divine Pulse rewind (FE: Three Houses) | Sands of Time rewind (Prince of Persia) |
| **Squad Size** | 4 active (swappable) | 4 active + 2 reserve (grid-limited) | 4 active (real-time tag) |
| **Positioning** | Snap-to-slots on shared grid | Height/facing/terrain on shared grid | Free movement within arena bounds |
| **Camera** | Golden Sun swoop (high → low) | Isometric top-down lock | Third-person tracking with Phantom Camera |
| **Clock Integration** | 48-min clock drives encounters, NPC schedules, shard visibility | 48-min clock drives AI aggression, terrain hazards | 48-min clock drives enemy spawn density, elemental shifts |
| **Timegates** | Core traversal mechanic (5 eras) | Easter eggs / lore relics / side-quests | Easter eggs / lore relics / side-quests |
| **Primary Tone** | Wonder, discovery, temporal mystery | Consequence, strategy, political weight | Urgency, fluidity, visceral impact |

**Shared Grid Rule:** All three games take place on the same physical space. No battle scene transitions. The camera swoops, the state machine flips, combat begins where you stand.

## 3. THE 48-MINUTE CLOCK

**Structure:** 48 real-time minutes = 1 full in-game day cycle. Split into four 12-minute phases:

| Phase | Time | Lighting | Enemy Behavior | Music |
|-------|------|----------|----------------|-------|
| **Dawn** | 00:00–11:59 | Warm gold, long shadows, mist burning off | Passive. Patrols slow. Some enemies dormant. | Soft, hopeful. Lower BPM. Acoustic/strings forward. |
| **Day** | 12:00–23:59 | Full sun, crisp visibility | Standard aggression. Full spawn tables. | Full arrangement. Standard BPM. All stems active. |
| **Dusk** | 24:00–35:59 | Orange/purple. Shadows lengthen. | Increased patrol speed. Ambush chance rises. | Tempo shifts. Minor key swap. Percussion drops. |
| **Night** | 36:00–47:59 | Dark blue/silver. Volumetric fog. | Hyper-aggressive. Vision cones expand 50%. Rare spawns. | Darker arrangement. Kajiura-style pads. Slower BPM. |

**Rules:**
- Clock **pauses** during combat, dialogue, cutscenes, and menus.
- Clock **advances** via resting (camp/inn/tent), fast travel, or specific story triggers.
- Some events are **phase-locked** (e.g., Chrono Shard III only visible at Dusk in a specific alley).
- The clock is **real-time** but can be modded via `TimeManager.PHASE_DURATION` for accessibility.

## 4. STAT SYSTEM — THE ADSR/PWM MATRIX

**Core Stats (4 primary + 2 derived):**

| Stat Name | Synth Origin | RPG Function | Mechanical Role |
|-----------|-------------|--------------|-----------------|
| **Attack** | ADSR: Attack / PWM: Pulse Width | Raw offensive output | Base weapon damage × proficiency multiplier. Governs burst potential and armor penetration. |
| **Decay** | ADSR: Decay / PWM: Roll-off | Damage mitigation | Reduces incoming damage over time. Governs armor durability, status tick resistance, and stagger recovery speed. |
| **Sustain** | ADSR: Sustain / PWM: Duty Cycle | Endurance pool | Total stamina/mana capacity. Governs how long you can maintain combat actions, sprint, or channel spells before depletion. |
| **Release** | ADSR: Release / PWM: Modulation Depth | Critical/Elemental output | Governs crit chance, elemental burst damage, status application rate, and post-combat regen speed. |
| **Resonance** *(derived)* | Harmonic overtone | Synergy multiplier | Scales with affinity bonds, instrument-weapon combos, and party positioning. Multiplies team-wide output. |
| **Tempo** *(derived)* | BPM / Clock speed | Action economy | Governs turn order (turn-based), action gauge fill rate (real-time), and movement range (tactical). |

**Stat Growth:**
- Stats grow via combat use, job evolution, and mentorship bonds.
- No traditional "level up." Instead: **Proficiency Tiers** (Apprentice → Journeyman → Artisan → Master → Transcendent).
- Each tier unlocks new weapon skills, job evolutions, or instrument combos.
- The Elf Princess caps at **85% proficiency** across all 4 weapons (her inexperience flaw), while dedicated specialists hit 130–150%.

**UI Presentation:**
- Stats displayed as waveform visualizations (ADSR envelope shapes).
- Hovering a stat shows the synth glyph + a 2-line explanation.
- Players who know synthesis get the wink. Everyone else sees clean RPG numbers.

## 5. CLASS/JOB SYSTEM

**Design Principles:**
- No "Novice" class. Every character starts with a **Base Job** that reflects their narrative role.
- Jobs **evolve** (Ragnarok Online tree) but can be **swapped freely** in and out of combat (FFV/X-2/Bravely Default).
- Every job has **2 weapon slots** (standard party) or **4 weapon slots** (Elowen only).
- Fluid combat swapping via AnimationTree blend states. No loading, no leaving the grid.

**Job Evolution Tree (Example Branch):**

```
Base: Warden (Tank/Protector)
  ├─ Tier 2: Shield Maiden (Defensive specialist)
  │    ├─ Tier 3: Bulwark (Pure tank, taunt/aggro)
  │    └─ Tier 3: Spellbreaker (Anti-magic tank)
  ├─ Tier 2: Vanguard (Offensive tank)
  │    ├─ Tier 3: Juggernaut (Heavy AoE)
  │    └─ Tier 3: Duelist (Single-target burst)
  └─ Tier 2: Tidekeeper (Support tank, hydration/thermal routing)
       ├─ Tier 3: Anchor (Party-wide defense buff)
       └─ Tier 3: Current-Weaver (Terrain manipulation)
```

**Weapon Slot Rules:**

| Character Type | Slots | Proficiency Cap | Rationale |
|----------------|-------|-----------------|-----------|
| Elowen (Protagonist) | 4 + Magic | 85% per weapon | Versatility over mastery. Reflects inexperience. Adapts to any boss. |
| Standard Party Member | 2 + Magic | 130–150% per weapon | Depth over breadth. Masters their tools. |
| Mentor/NPC Allies | 1–2 | 100–120% | Focused. Reflects their cultural specialization. |

**In-Combat Swap Mechanic:**
- Hold swap button → time slows to 20% → radial Weapon Wheel appears → select → release → 0.3s animation blend → stats recalculate → immediate action.
- For Elowen: 4-slice wheel. For party: 2-slice toggle.
- AnimationTree BlendSpace handles the visual transition seamlessly.

## 6. COMBAT SYSTEMS — DETAILED

### Game 1: Turn-Based (Chrono Trigger Model)

- **Turn Order:** Determined by `Tempo` stat. Displayed as a timeline bar (CT-style).
- **Positioning:** Characters snap to `Marker3D` slots. Enemies occupy physical space. AoE attacks hit based on spatial overlap.
- **Dual/Triple Techs:** When affinity between 2+ party members exceeds threshold, combined attacks unlock. Triggered via adjacent positioning + simultaneous action selection.
- **Elemental Pairing:** Weapons carry elemental tags. Combining elements on the same target triggers hybrid effects (Fire + Wind = Plasma Burn, Water + Earth = Mudslide, etc.).
- **Timegate Combat:** Some bosses fight *across* eras. Phase 1 in Antiquity → Phase 2 shifts to Industrial. Arena changes mid-fight.

### Game 2: Tactical (Triangle Strategy Model)

- **Grid:** The overworld terrain *is* the grid. Height, facing, cover, and elemental terrain all matter.
- **Movement:** NavigationRegion3D pathfinding. Ghost tiles show valid range. Click to move.
- **Divine Pulse:** 3 charges per battle. Rewinds 1 full turn. UI shows a "page turning backward" animation.
- **Height Advantage:** Attacking from higher ground grants +15% damage. Backstab (hitting the rear Area3D) grants critical.
- **Formation Bonuses:** Specific party arrangements unlock passive buffs (e.g., diamond formation = +Decay, line formation = +Tempo).

### Game 3: Real-Time (Trials of Mana Model)

- **Movement:** Free within arena bounds (Area3D container). Camera tracks via Phantom Camera.
- **Attack System:** Light/heavy chains via AnimationPlayer hitbox activation. Weapon hitboxes are Area3Ds toggled on/off during animation frames.
- **Dodge:** I-frames via CollisionShape3D disable during dodge animation (0.3s window).
- **Charge Attacks:** Hold attack → charge animation → release for amplified damage. Getting hit interrupts.
- **Sands of Time Rewind:** Hold rewind → last 10 seconds of position/animation play backward. Sepia filter + VHS audio effect. Limited by a "Sand Gauge" that refills over time.
- **Tag System:** Swap active character instantly. AI controls inactive members with basic patrol/attack routines.

## 7. SOCIAL SIM & AFFINITY

**Core Loop:**
1. **Camp/Tavern/Inn** → Interact with party members
2. **Gift Giving** → Present food/items → Likes/Dislikes trigger affinity changes
3. **Dialogue** → Branching conversations via Dialogic 2. Choices affect affinity, lore access, and romance flags.
4. **Combat Synergy** → High affinity unlocks Dual Techs, formation bonuses, and instrument combo amplifiers.
5. **Mentorship** → Cross-gender training bonds. Formalized via rituals. Scales with shared combat time.

**Affinity Tiers:**

| Tier | Threshold | Unlocks |
|------|-----------|---------|
| Stranger | 0–19 | Basic dialogue. Standard combat AI. |
| Acquaintance | 20–39 | Camp banter. Minor stat synergy (+1%). |
| Companion | 40–59 | Support conversations. Dual Tech access. |
| Confidant | 60–79 | Deep lore. Romance option opens. +3% synergy. Instrument combos. |
| Bonded | 80–99 | Full romance/platonic bond. Legacy traits. +5% synergy. Unique ending flags. |
| Transcendent | 100 | Secret post-credits content. Cross-game continuity bonuses. |

**Romance Philosophy:**
- Romance is **optional** and **never mandatory** for progression.
- Any gender pairing is valid. No BL/GL gate. Affinity tracks `Combat Sync`, `Camp Trust`, and `Romance Interest` as separate axes.
- A fully bonded platonic friendship provides identical mechanical bonuses to a romance.
- The player can pursue multiple bonds but only one "Transcendent" bond per playthrough.

## 8. THE FOUR ENDINGS — STRUCTURE

| Ending | Trigger | Tone | Reward |
|--------|---------|------|--------|
| **Bad (The Pun)** | Use a specific skill exactly 100 times, then confront final boss. | Absurdist comedy. Party transforms into kitchen countertops. | Steam Achievement: "Formica Knight." Trading Card. |
| **Good (Standard)** | Beat final boss without True/Secret criteria met. | Bittersweet. Standard JRPG wrap-up. | GlobalProfile flag. Standard credits. |
| **True (Time Manipulator)** | Collect all 5 Chrono Shards (phase-locked, era-specific). Defeat the *real* final boss (Phase 2). | Epic. Reveals the 48-minute clock's true creator. Sawano choir kicks in. | Unlocks post-credits sequence check. |
| **Secret/Real (Cross-Game Hook)** | True Ending + obscure criteria (bury the Rusty Gear in Antiquity, spare the Midnight NPC, rest at a specific camp during Night phase). | Mind-bending. Post-credits glitch. Sets up Game 2. | GlobalProfile flag carries to Game 2. Alters opening cinematic. Unlocks secret character. |

**Sequel Hook Mechanic:**
- The Secret Ending writes to `GlobalProfile.json` (saved to Documents folder, shared across all 3 games).
- Game 2 reads this on boot. If `Secret_Ending_Seen == true`, the opening cinematic changes, a secret character joins the roster, and specific world-state flags alter terrain/NPC placement.

## 9. AUDIO DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

**Composer Inspirations (not fusion, but *vocabulary*):**
- **Motoi Sakuraba** → Warm symphonic prog rock. Hammond organ. Odd time signatures. Battle themes.
- **Nobuo Uematsu** → Fast-paced progressive fantasy. Memorable melodies. Boss themes.
- **Yuki Kajiura** → Ethereal vocal pads. Intimate strings. Emotional beats. Night/exploration themes.
- **Hiroyuki Sawano** → Epic hybrid. Massive taiko. Choir. Phase 2 boss climaxes.
- **Falcom Sound Team jdk** → High-BPM jazz/rock fusion. Town themes. Energetic exploration.

**Adaptive Audio Rules:**
- All tracks exported as **stems** (Rhythm, Harmony, Melody, Ambience, Vocals).
- **FFXIV Model:** Time-of-day shifts crossfade stems. Dawn = acoustic forward. Night = pads forward, percussion drops.
- **Halo Model:** Spatial layering. Caves muffle via LowPass filter. Dense forests mute percussion, boost strings. Combat swells rhythm + brass.
- **Boss Escalation:** Boss music is the area theme "taken one step further." Same key, same melody, but heavier instrumentation, faster tempo, added choir.
- **Instrument-Weapon Synergy:** Rhythm inputs during combat trigger temporary stat buffs AND shift the audio mix (e.g., landing a drum-beat combo brings the percussion stem forward for 8 seconds).

**VST Toolkit:**
- Arturia Pigments (synths, pads, arps)
- Arturia B-3 V (Sakuraba organ)
- Ample Sound / Shreddage 3 (guitars)
- Spitfire Audio (orchestra, chamber strings)
- Heavyocity DAMAGE 2 / Vocalise (Sawano percussion, Kajiura vocals)
- Spectrasonics Trilian / Modo Bass (Falcom slap bass)
- GetGood Drums / Steven Slate Drums (rock kits)
- SWAM Saxophones (jdk brass)

## 10. ANTI-CHEAT & ANTI-PIRACY PHILOSOPHY

**Design Principle:** Never crash. Never accuse. **Gaslight.**

| Layer | Mechanism | Pirate Experience |
|-------|-----------|-------------------|
| **Memory Obfuscation** | SecureInt (XOR encryption + key rotation) | Cheat Engine sees garbage values. |
| **Honey Pot** | If HP jumps by >200 in one frame, flag `is_compromised` | Silent trigger. No notification. |
| **Cursed RNG** | Damage reduced 10–30%. Crits heal enemies. | "This game is really hard." |
| **UI Gaslight** | Text misspells. Health bars drift. Tooltips lie. | "Is this a bug?" |
| **Audio Detune** | Master bus pitch slowly drops as corruption_level rises. | Soundtrack sounds "off." |
| **Beacon Misdirection** | Waypoint beacons spawn 50m from actual objective. | "The guide is wrong." |
| **Ambush Spikes** | Resting has 20% ambush chance (0% for legitimate players). | "Sleeping is risky." |

**Legitimate players never see any of this.** The game plays perfectly clean.

## 11. ACCESSIBILITY & QoL

- **Pause & Save Anywhere.** No exceptions. Combat, cutscenes, dialogue—all pausable.
- **UI Scaling:** Slider from 0.75x to 2.0x. Steam Deck native support.
- **Colorblind Modes:** Protanopia, Deuteranopia, Tritanopia shaders. Toggleable.
- **Controller Navigation:** Full focus-mode support. D-pad/stick navigates all menus.
- **Subtitle Options:** Size, background opacity, speaker nameplates.
- **Combat Speed:** 1x, 1.5x, 2x toggle for turn-based and tactical.
- **Clock Accessibility:** Optional "Extended Day" mode (96-minute cycle) for players who want slower pacing.
- **No Motion Sickness Triggers:** Camera swoops have adjustable speed. Screen shake toggleable.

## 12. MODDING PHILOSOPHY

**Core Rule:** Modders need a text editor and a folder. No Godot. No compiling.

- All game data (items, enemies, dialogue, quests) stored in external **JSON/CSV**.
- `user://mods/` folder scanned on boot. Valid `manifest.json` = mod loads.
- Dialogic timelines export as `.dtl` (JSON). Drop them in a mod folder.
- Steam Workshop integration via GodotSteam UGC.
- Mod-safe variables documented. Anti-tamper variables are **locked**.
- Community can create: balance patches, new items, custom dialogue, scavenger hunts, joke endings.

## 1. COSMOLOGY & PLANETARY HISTORY

**The Planet: Eldoria**
- Sylvani name: *Aethel-Vael* ("The Breathing Root")
- Solani name: *Terra-Nova* ("The New Shore")
- Human name: *Eldoria* (adopted post-Seed Ship era)

**Origin:**
Eldoria is a **testing facility** built by a Precursor race that went extinct for unknown reasons. The planet's ecosystem, leyline network, and native inhabitants were left to evolve autonomously. The Precursors' technology now reads as "lost mysticism" to modern races.

**The Seed Ship Event (Era 1):**
Humans crash-landed via a **Seed Ship** (not a colony vessel—a genetic ark). Stranded with no way home, they adapted over thousands of years, discovered the planet's ambient mana (part of the ecosystem), warred with native races, and eventually **forgot their own heritage and technology**. Their tech became legend, then myth, then "magic."

**The Ley Veins:**
Subterranean mana rivers connecting all continents. They pulse with planetary rhythm. The 48-minute clock is a *surface expression* of leyline tidal cycles. Spirits, elementals, and dragons are sustained by leyline flow. When the leylines die, so does magic.

| Era | Name | Aesthetic | Key Conflict | Timegate Access |
|-----|------|-----------|--------------|-----------------|
| **Era 1** | The Primordial Dawn | Sci-fi ruins overgrown with organic life. Seed ship wreckage. Proto-magic discovery. | Humans vs. native races. Survival. Adaptation. | Game 1 only (core mechanic) |
| **Era 2** | The Dark Age | War-torn. Fortified cities. Ash skies. Vorashi first surge. | All races vs. Vorashi. Sylvani Aetheric Implosion. | Game 1 + Game 2 side-quests |

**Era Transition Rules:**
- The world physically changes between eras. Same grid, different props/lighting/NPC populations.

- The `WorldStateManager` handles era swaps by toggling Node3D folders and swapping WorldEnvironment.

## 4. THE MANA SYSTEM

**Core Principle:** Mana is atmospheric planetary energy stored biologically in **Aetheric Loci** (Western races) or circulated via **Meridian Pathways** (Eastern races). Same resource. Different routing.

**Storage & Growth:**
- Age = capacity. Older beings store exponentially more mana.
- Bloodline/genes = voltage efficiency and elemental affinity.
- Elves are "rechargeable mana batteries." Humans rely more on brute force/stamina/cunning.

**The Four Base Elements:**

| Element | Domain | Eastern Equivalent |
|---------|--------|--------------------|
| **Ignis** (Fire) | Heat, combustion, plasma, passion | Huǒ |
| **Terran** (Earth) | Density, pressure, growth, stone | Tǔ |
| **Aqua** (Water) | Fluidity, emotion, cold, life | Shuǐ |
| **Zephyr** (Wind) | Motion, space, breath, time | Fēng |

| Combination | Result | Rarity |
|-------------|--------|--------|

**Eastern Cultivation (Xianxia from Western POV):**

## 6. THE VORASHI (Parasitic Entities)

**Nature:** Extraterrestrial parasitic organisms. Crash-landed **globally** millennia ago. Not one invasion—multiple landings across all continents.

**Behavior:**
- Feed on **negative energy and emotions** (fear, rage, sorrow, despair).
- Multiply in emotional turmoil. Dormant in stable/peaceful regions.
- 90% of biomass is **aquatic**. Oceans, lakes, rivers, underground aquifers.
- "Dark Water" zones are permanently marked on naval charts.
- Land contact is temporary. They migrate toward moisture and emotional resonance.

**The Sylvani Catastrophe:**
- ~300 years after Elowen vanished, a concentrated Vorashi surge hit Kaelen-Værd via a fractured leyline node.
- The Sylvani, isolationist and unprepared, suffered catastrophic losses.
- The Matriarch Council triggered the **Aetheric Implosion**: a continent-wide leyline collapse using synchronized Solani mana-capacitors + ancient reversal rites.
- Half the western seaboard was obliterated. All participating casters died.
- Residual Vorashi colonies linger in the scarred ruins.

**Other Races' Preparedness:**
- Solani: Coastal early-warning systems. Naval blockades.
- Humans: Fortified enclaves. Fire-walls. Artillery.
- Eastern clans: Geomantic arrays. Cultivation wards.
- Gharuk: Heat-vents. Sand-burial tactics. Rapid relocation.

**The Looming Threat: Ouroboros-Prime**
- A dormant planetary consciousness that feeds on purified mana.
- If Eldoria is "cleaned" too aggressively, Ouroboros wakes hungry.
- The 48-minute clock is partly a *containment rhythm* keeping it dormant.
- Elowen's mission: purify selectively without triggering mass awakening.

## 10. THE LANGUAGE OF MAGIC & MUSIC

- Attack = initial mana burst. Decay = falloff rate. Sustain = held channel. Release = termination bloom.
- PWM (Pulse Width Modulation) governs the *duty cycle* of sustained spells—how long the mana "stays on" before cycling.

**The Cowbell Running Joke:**
- A `Sprite3D` cowbell hidden in every single hub across all three games.
- Interacting plays a crisp *CLANK* and increments a hidden counter.
- At 50: Unlock "More Cowbell" achievement.
- At 100: A secret NPC appears who gives you a legendary cowbell weapon.
- At 150: The credits theme is replaced with a cowbell concerto.

**Musical Puzzles:**
- Precursor Synthesizers in dungeons. Push blocks onto pressure plates to play notes.
- Match the hidden chord → door opens.
- Inspired by Alundra, Golden Sun, DKC3 Banana Bird quest.

### 🌑 THE MECHANIC: AETHERIC BLIGHT (VORASHI RESONANCE)
Instead of a "Good vs. Evil" bar, let's call it **Resonance (Pure)** vs. **Dissonance (Corrupted)**. 
*   **How you gain it:** Using corrupted Precursor tech, resting in Dark Water zones, executing ruthless/traumatic combat moves (which feeds the Vorashi), or taking heavy hits from Vorashi bosses.
*   **How you cleanse it:** Purifying Leyline nodes, playing specific "Pure Chords" on Precursor Synths (your music puzzles!), or using rare, expensive Pure Aether at camp.

#### 1. Combat & ADSR Stat Shifts (The "Stronger/Weaker" Loop)
Corruption acts as a dangerous, addictive buff that slowly cripples your survivability. It directly warps your ADSR/PWM stats.

| Stat | Pure State (0-40% Corruption) | Tainted State (41-79% Corruption) | Blighted State (80-99% Corruption) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Attack** (Burst) | Normal baseline. | **+15%**. Vorashi energy makes your strikes vicious and heavy. | **+30%**. You hit like a truck, but your animations look feral/jagged. |
| **Decay** (Mitigation) | Normal armor/damage reduction. | **-15%**. Your aura makes you a beacon; enemies target you more, armor degrades faster. | **-30%**. You are a glass cannon. Enemies aggressively swarm you. |
| **Sustain** (Stamina) | Normal stamina pool. | **-20%**. Dark energy burns through your biological reserves. | **-40%**. You gasp for breath between combos. Sprinting drains you rapidly. |
| **Release** (Crit/Element) | Standard elemental pairing. | **Shifted Elements.** Wind becomes *Plasma/Void*. Water becomes *Acid/Dark Water*. | **Volatile.** High crit chance, but 10% chance to damage *yourself* on a miss. |

*Protagonist Tie-in:* Elowen’s **Precursor Gauntlet** runs *better* on corruption, but her biological weapons (Sword/Spear/Bow) suffer. Her tsundere personality also shifts—her dialogue gets sharper, more isolated, and her `Frustration/Overdrive` mechanic triggers much easier.

#### 2. World Reactivity & "Silent Fails" (The Gaslight)
This is where your design shines. No giant red "QUEST FAILED" text. The world just naturally rejects you.

*   **NPCs & Hubs:** If you walk into a Sylvani grove with high Dissonance, the ambient music drops its Kajiura-style vocals and becomes dissonant. NPCs physically turn away, cross their arms, or leave the room. The Tavern Keeper won't look you in the eye.
*   **Shops & Economy:** Merchants don't just say "I hate you." They say, *"Your aura is souring the milk, traveler. I only have the dregs left."* They quietly swap out high-tier healing items for diluted, weaker versions, or hike prices by 300%. You are locked out of the *good* gear without a explicit lock icon.
*   **The 48-Minute Clock:** High corruption makes the **Night Phase** terrifying. Enemy vision cones expand by 80%, and Vorashi ambushes become almost guaranteed if you try to sleep in the wild.
*   **Silent Quest Fails:** 
    *   *Example:* You tell a refugee family to hide in a specific cave. If your corruption is high, the Vorashi track *your* scent to that cave. You return three in-game days later to give them supplies, and the cave is empty, covered in dark water stains, with a single torn doll on the floor. The quest just quietly updates to "Objective: Move On." 
    *   *Example:* A Beastkin scout refuses to track a monster for you because your scent "burns his nose." You have to find the monster yourself, blind, without the waypoint beacon.

#### 3. Purification & The Campfire Loop
Purification shouldn't just be a menu click; it should be a ritual that ties into your camp mechanics and mentorship system.

## ⚖️ THE CREED AXIS: THRESHOLD SYSTEM

Think Bioware's reputation tiers meets Bethesda's faction locks, but with **weighted choices** so every decision has texture.

### Scoring Philosophy

Not every choice is +1 or -1. Choices carry **weight classes**:

| Weight | Point Value | Examples |
|--------|-------------|----------|
| **Micro** | ±1–3 | Small dialogue flavour, minor NPC courtesy, picking up litter |
| **Standard** | ±5–8 | Quest resolution style, sparing/executing a minor foe, trade negotiation outcome |
| **Major** | ±12–18 | Faction allegiance shifts, sparing/killing a named NPC, sacrificing a resource hub |
| **Pivotal** | ±25–35 | Era-altering decisions, betraying a bonded ally, triggering/avoiding a continental event |

A single Pivotal choice can swing you across two thresholds in one moment. That's the Bioware "one conversation changes everything" energy.

### Threshold Tiers

| Tier | Range | Title | Unlocks/Locks |
|------|-------|-------|---------------|
| **0** | -20 to +20 | **Wanderer** | Baseline. All factions neutral. Standard dialogue. No special access. |

| **2** | +41 to +60 | **Advocate** | Faction quests open. Companion trust conversations deepen. Dual Techs gain +5% synergy. Some enemy encounters offer "talk down" options. |
| **3** | +61 to +80 | **Champion** | Major faction alliances. Unique gear/recipes unlock. NPCs in aligned zones greet you warmly. Some hostile zones become traversable without combat. |
| **4** | +81 to +100 | **Paragon** | Secret ending criteria partially met. Exclusive post-game content. Aligned faction fights *for* you in scripted encounters. Unique ultimate ability unlocked. |
| **-1** | -21 to -40 | **Doubter** | Some shopkeepers wary. 1–2 quests close. Companions question your methods. |
| **-2** | -41 to -60 | **Antagonist** | Aligned factions hostile. Locked out of certain hubs. Companion trust erodes. Enemy encounters gain ambush chance. |

### The "Every Option Matters" Rule

## 🌑 CORRUPTION: THE OVERPOWERED-FRAGILE CURVE

You want the player to feel like a **glass god**. Devastating output, but the world can kill them with a sneeze. Not Dante Must Die, but *uncomfortably close* at high corruption.

### Scaling Philosophy

Corruption isn't a flat debuff. It's an **exponential trade-off curve**. Every 10% bracket shifts the fantasy:

| Corruption % | Attack/Release Output | Decay/Sustain (Defence/Stamina) | Healing Received | Enemy Aggression | The Fantasy |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---|
| **0–10%** | Baseline | Baseline | 100% | Baseline | Clean. Balanced. The standard JRPG experience. |
| **11–20%** | +10% | -5% | 95% | +10% vision cone | Slightly reckless. You hit a bit harder but feel the edges soften. |
| **21–35%** | +25% | -15% | 85% | +20% | Noticeably aggressive. Enemies swarm you more. Stamina drains faster in combos. |
| **36–50%** | +40% | -25% | 70% | +35% | The "glass" starts showing. You one-shot things that used to take three hits, but a bad dodge costs you a chunk of HP. |
| **51–65%** | +60% | -35% | 55% | +50% | **The sweet spot.** You feel like a raid boss. Every crit is a spectacle. But one missed dodge against a heavy attack drops you to 20% HP. |
| **66–80%** | +85% | -50% | 40% | +65% | Overpowered and visibly frail. Enemies target you over AI allies. Healing items are almost useless. You're playing on a knife's edge. |
| **81–95%** | +110% | -65% | 25% | +80% | **The glass god.** You hit like a continent. A single standard enemy hit takes 40-50% of your HP. Boss AoEs can one-shot you if you're not mid-dodge. |
| **96–100%** | +130% | -75% | 10% | +100% (all enemies aggro on sight) | **Death's door.** You're a walking nuke that shatters if you breathe wrong. Enemies spawn faster, hit harder, and never stop coming. Healing is essentially zero. One hit = death. |

### The "Between Dante Must Die and Normal" Sweet Spot

At **66–80% corruption**, the player should feel like they're playing a harder difficulty tier, but one *they chose* through their actions. Key tuning:

- **Enemy damage multiplier at 70% corruption:** roughly **1.8x** (not the 4x of Dante Must Die, but enough that a standard mob hit feels threatening)
- **Player output at 70% corruption:** roughly **2.2x** (you're obliterating things, but you can't tank the return fire)
- **Boss encounters at 70%+:** Boss AoEs become near-lethal. You *must* dodge, reposition, or use party members as bait. Standing still and trading hits is suicide.
- **The "oh shit" moment:** At 80%+, a *normal walking enemy* can take you from 100% to 30% HP in one hit. You start avoiding encounters you'd normally walk into. The overworld becomes tense.

### Combat Feel at High Corruption

| Element | Low Corruption (0–30%) | Mid Corruption (40–60%) | High Corruption (70–100%) |
|---------|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **Normal attacks** | Standard hit → enemy staggers | Enemy launches on hit | Enemy *explodes* on hit. Overkill damage splashes to nearby foes. |
| **Crits** | 1.5x damage, flash effect | 2x damage, screen shake | 3x damage, brief slow-mo, enemy ragdolls. But the recoil drains your stamina by 15%. |
| **Elemental bursts** | Standard pairing effects | Pairing effects + AoE splash | Pairing effects + massive AoE, but 10% chance to damage *yourself* from the backlash. |
| **Taking a hit** | Standard damage number, brief flinch | Larger damage number, longer flinch | Massive damage number, full stagger, screen cracks. If HP drops below 20%, vision blurs and audio distorts. |
| **Dodge window** | Standard 0.3s i-frames | Unchanged | Unchanged. You still have the same dodge. But the punishment for missing it is *severe*. |

### The "Feeble" Fantasy in Practice

At high corruption, the game should make you feel **powerful but hunted**:

- **Enemies prioritise you.** AI targeting shifts. Even if your party has a tank, enemies path toward the corrupted player.
- **Ambush frequency increases.** Roaming enemies on the overworld detect you from further away. At 80%+, enemies you'd normally sneak past will turn and charge.
- **Resting is risky.** At 60%+ corruption, resting at camp has a 15% ambush chance. At 80%+, it's 35%. The world doesn't let you sleep peacefully.
- **NPCs react.** At 50%+, shopkeepers start refusing service. At 70%+, civilians flee when you enter a hub. At 90%+, guards attack on sight in aligned zones.
- **Your own party notices.** Companions comment on your state. At 40%, your Gentle Anchor says, *"Your veins are glowing again."* At 70%, your Stoic Paragon quietly positions themselves between you and every enemy. At 90%, they stop talking to you entirely.

### The Purification Counterweight

Because high corruption is *so* punishing, purification needs to feel **earned and relieving**:

| Purification Method | Corruption Cleared | Cost/Risk |
|---|---|---|
| **Leyline Node Cleansing** | -15% per node | Requires solving a music puzzle. Takes 2 in-game hours. |
| **Precursor Synth Chord** | -10% | One-use. Found in dungeons. Limited supply. |
| **Camp Meditation (Pure Aether)** | -5% per session | Costs 1 Pure Aether item. Takes 30 min of game time. |
| **Companion Bond Ritual** | -8% | Requires Companion at Tier 3+ affinity. Once per companion per playthrough. |
| **Timegate Immersion** | -20% | Only in Game 1. Requires activating a Timegate and waiting through a full phase shift. |

### UI/Visual Feedback for Corruption

The player should *see and hear* their corruption without opening a menu:

- **0–30%:** No visual changes. Clean.
- **31–50%:** Faint dark veins on the character sprite's arms. Slight audio detune on hit sounds.
- **51–70%:** Veins glow dark purple. Character idle animation adds a subtle twitch. Music gains a low dissonant hum layer.
- **71–90%:** Full vein glow. Eyes shift colour. Idle animation becomes restless. Music dissonance is noticeable. NPCs visibly react.
- **91–100%:** Character sprite has a dark aura. Every footstep leaves a brief shadow trail. Music is noticeably warped. Screen edges darken subtly. Companions keep physical distance in camp.

## ⚔️ COMBAT: CORRUPTION NULLIFIES CORRUPTION

This is the mechanical heart of the system. The elemental advantage system we built (Ignis, Terran, Aqua, Zephyr + hybrids) operates on a simple rule: **matching elements neutralize, opposing elements amplify.**

### The Nullification Rule

| Your Element vs. Enemy Element | Result |
|:---|:---|
| **Opposing** (e.g., your Aqua vs. enemy Ignis) | **Amplified damage.** Standard elemental advantage. +25% damage. |
| **Matching** (e.g., your Aqua vs. enemy Aqua) | **Nullified.** Elemental bonus is zero. Pure stat struggle. ATK vs DEC. No multiplier. |
| **Corruption vs. Corruption** | **Hard nullified.** All elemental bonuses are zero. Both parties lose elemental affinity entirely. It becomes a pure ATK/DEC/Sustain/Release stat comparison. |
| **Corruption vs. Non-Corruption** | Corrupted attacker gains **+15% ATK** against non-corrupted targets (fear/terror bonus) but **-10% DEC** (you're reckless, they're desperate). |
| **Non-Corruption vs. Corruption** | Non-corrupted attacker gains **+10% DEC** (defensive resolve) but **-15% ATK** (hesitation/fear penalty). |

### What This Means Tactically

**At low corruption (0–30%):**
- You fight corrupted enemies with elemental advantage. Standard JRPG tactics. Exploit weaknesses.
- Corruption is a *debuff* you want to cleanse.

**At mid corruption (40–60%):**
- You start losing elemental advantage against corrupted enemies. Your matching element is nullified.
- You're stronger in raw output but losing tactical flexibility.
- Corruption is becoming a *trade-off*.

**At high corruption (70–100%):**
- Against corrupted enemies, **all elemental advantages vanish.** It's a pure power struggle. Your ATK vs their DEC. Your Sustain vs their Release.
- You hit like a god. But so do they. And you're fragile.
- Against non-corrupted enemies, you're terrifying. They hesitate. You don't.
- Corruption is now a **deliberate combat stance.** You *choose* to be corrupted to fight corrupted enemies on even ground, sacrificing your elemental toolkit for raw power.

### The "Power Struggle" Visual

When corruption nullifies corruption in combat:

- Both your elemental aura and the enemy's elemental aura **shatter simultaneously** with a glass-break SFX.
- The screen flashes white for 0.3 seconds.
- Both combatants are stripped to their base stat visual—no elemental glow, no particle effects. Just raw, brutal physical/magical output.
- The UI damage numbers switch from elemental-colored to **pure white** with a heavy impact font.
- The music drops all melodic elements. Only percussion remains. A war drum. Two fighters. No magic. Just force.

### Boss Encounters at High Corruption

At 80%+ corruption, when you fight a **corrupted boss:**

- The boss's elemental phases are **disabled.** No fire phase, no water phase. Just raw, escalating attacks.
- The boss AI switches to "desperate survival" mode. It fights like it's fighting *you*, the raid boss.
- The boss's health bar appears at the **top of the screen** (standard), but YOUR health bar also appears at the top, next to it. Two boss bars. The game treats this as a **boss vs. boss fight.**
- The music is a dueling arrangement—your boss theme and the enemy's boss theme layered over each other, fighting for dominance. Whichever side is winning gets the louder stem.

## ⏯️ PLAYBACK CONTROLS (The Tape Machine)

A dive is a **Reel**: a fixed recorded span (minutes to an in-game day), played on the same physical grid via your Base+Modifier folders (`Props_Reel_<id>`).

- **Scrub** — fast-forward/rewind the recording. Clues live at different timestamps: the key is hidden at minute 3, the name is spoken at minute 7, the murderer's face catches light at minute 12. *Scrubbing is exploration.*
- **Pause & walk** — freeze the echo-tableau mid-moment and inspect positions, objects, shadows (feeds your statue-shadow and light-alignment puzzles).
- **Loop point** — set an A/B loop to re-watch a fast moment frame-by-frame.
- **Wow & Flutter meter (0–100%)** — degradation from re-diving the same reel (past 3 dives: +5% and +0.05 paradox each), phase mismatch, and scrub-spam. At 100%: **tape dropout** — the reel collapses to static and locks until a leyline purification rite "cleans the tape." Purification doubles as restoration. Chef's kiss synergy.
- **Black Reels** — traumatic memories (fear, rage, grief) are **Vorashi-infested**, because the parasites feed on exactly that. Diving one means the still-moment is *crawling*: memory-combat encounters against residue-Vorashi. High risk, deepest clues.
